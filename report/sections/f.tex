\paragraph{Test sample and annotation.}
The error analysis in this section uses the initial RoBERTa ensemble,
trained with a constant learning rate and evaluated at a threshold of
0.680. After excluding 101 test reviews whose text also appeared in
the training set, we selected 40 reviews for manual annotation.
Fifteen were closest to the initial RoBERTa decision threshold,
15 were drawn from reviews on which that ensemble and TF-IDF + SVM
disagreed, and 10 were drawn at random from the remaining reviews.
Two members labeled all 40 independently and agreed on 38 (95\%).
A third member resolved the two disagreements.

\paragraph{Correct and incorrect predictions.}
RoBERTa matched the final human labels on 31 of the 40 sampled reviews
(0.775). The sample deliberately included reviews near the decision
threshold and reviews on which the models disagreed, so this accuracy
applies only to the selected reviews. On the same 40 reviews, the final RoBERTa ensemble described in
Section~\ref{sec:results} matched 34 human labels (0.850).

RoBERTa correctly classified reviews 274 and 99. In review 274, the
reviewer points out a limitation of the garment and still recommends
it for smoothing under clothes. That recommendation supports the
positive label. In review 99, the reviewer initially liked the bra's
shape and support, but the underwire eventually broke through. This
failure supports the negative label despite the initial praise.

RoBERTa classified review 549 as positive even though the reviewer
reported a broken boot strap and asked about returning the boots.
It classified review 1123 as negative. In that review, the cracking
and breaking described by the reviewer concerned the old plastic
luggage tags. The reviewer was satisfied with the new leather tag,
which was the product being evaluated.

\paragraph{Validation-set errors.}
On the shared validation set of 1,492 reviews, TF-IDF + SVM,
BiLSTM and RoBERTa misclassified 109, 104 and 69 reviews,
respectively. Of the 30 reviews that all three models
misclassified, 12 contained mixed sentiment. RoBERTa correctly
classified 66 reviews that SVM missed, including nine cases
categorized as involving context or negation. For example, in
validation review 523, the phrase ``not disappointed'' expresses
approval, and the reviewer ends with a recommendation.
